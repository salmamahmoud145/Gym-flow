Common questions

When does my plan start?

You choose your start date at checkout. The end date is automatically computed one calendar month later.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes — submit a freeze request and an admin will approve it within 24 hours. Your end date is extended by the frozen period.

What if I run out of credits early?

Your subscription expires automatically. You can renew at any time to get a fresh set of credits and a new end date.

Can I pay at the gym?

Yes. Choose "Pay at reception" at checkout. A staff member records your payment when you visit.
